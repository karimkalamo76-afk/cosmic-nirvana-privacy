Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Cosmic Nirvana is an automated content production project focused on creating relaxing space and astronomy-themed videos.

Information and API Access

The application uses Google APIs, including the YouTube Data API, solely to authenticate the authorized channel and upload videos generated by the application.

Use of Information

Information obtained through Google APIs is used only for the operation of the Cosmic Nirvana content publishing workflow. We do not sell or share personal information with third parties.

Data Storage

OAuth credentials and API credentials are stored securely as protected secrets and are not intentionally made public.

Contact

For questions about this application or this privacy policy, please contact the developer through the associated project account.
